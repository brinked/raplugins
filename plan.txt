RankAudit Optimization Platform
Content Optimizer + Product Optimizer + WordPress Integration

Master Product &amp; Development Plan

Purpose: Build a cloud-based RankAudit platform connected to Google Search Console and WordPress that
finds real organic-search opportunities, improves informational content and ecommerce product listings,
publishes controlled changes through a WordPress plugin, and measures the results.

1. Platform Vision
RankAudit should operate as the intelligence layer, while the WordPress plugin acts as a secure bridge
between RankAudit and the subscribers website. The system should avoid blind AI rewrites. It should use
verified data, actual Search Console performance, structured recommendations, controlled publishing,
revisions, and post-change measurement.
 Content Optimizer — improves articles, guides, blog posts and informational pages.
 Product Optimizer — improves WooCommerce product listings, specifications, descriptions and
purchase information.
 Trust Blocks — displays verified warranty, shipping, returns, stock, manufacturer and payment
information.
 Performance Tracking — measures rankings, impressions, clicks, CTR and the outcome of each
optimization.

2. Core Architecture
RankAudit Cloud Application
 User accounts, organizations, subscriptions and sites.
 Google OAuth and Google Search Console synchronization.
 WordPress site connections and secure authentication.
 Search opportunity detection and prioritization.
 Content/product analysis and AI generation.
 Background jobs and scheduled rechecks.
 Recommendation approval workflows.
 Change history, revisions, rollback records and audit logs.
 Performance reporting and notifications.
WordPress Plugin
 Pairs a WordPress site with the correct RankAudit account.
 Retrieves posts, pages, WooCommerce products, metadata and supported custom fields.
 Preserves Gutenberg blocks, shortcodes, embeds and important theme/plugin data.
 Creates drafts and revisions before changes.
 Applies approved changes and supports rollback.
 Displays RankAudit recommendations inside WordPress.
 Provides permission levels from analyze-only through optional autopilot.

3. Subscriber Onboarding
1. Create a RankAudit account and add a website.
2. Click Connect Google Search Console and complete Google OAuth.
3. Select the Search Console property associated with the site.
4. Install and activate the RankAudit WordPress plugin.
5. Pair the plugin using a one-time connection code.
6. Choose permissions: analyze only, create recommendations, create drafts, publish approved changes,
or full autopilot.
7. Run site verification and initial WordPress/Search Console synchronization.
8. Show the first opportunity dashboard after the initial data is processed.

4. Google Search Console Integration
Use Google OAuth 2.0 and begin with read-only Search Console access. Store refresh tokens encrypted on
the RankAudit server rather than inside WordPress. Allow users to revoke the connection at any time.
 Retrieve available Search Console properties after OAuth.
 Import query, page, country, device and search-appearance dimensions where useful.
 Store clicks, impressions, CTR and average position.
 Maintain rolling periods such as 7, 28, 90 days and longer historical windows where available.
 Store daily data so RankAudit can detect gains, declines, seasonality, cannibalization and post-
optimization changes.

5. WordPress Security &amp; Connection
 Prefer a plugin-issued, site-specific connection secret rather than requesting the user&#39;s WordPress
password.
 Sign sensitive requests with a site identifier, timestamp, nonce, request-body hash and HMAC signature.
 Verify capabilities and permissions before every mutation.
 Use HTTPS, secret rotation, rate limiting and audit logging.
 Create a WordPress revision before modifying published content.
 Provide immediate disconnect and rollback controls.
 WordPress Application Passwords can be supported as an alternative connection method.

PART I — CONTENT OPTIMIZER

6. Content Optimizer Goal

RankAudit — Master Content &amp; Product Optimization Plan

Find pages that already have a realistic chance to improve, diagnose why they may be underperforming,
recommend specific changes, create controlled WordPress drafts, and measure whether those changes
correlate with improved search performance.

7. Content Opportunity Engine
 Striking-distance pages, especially approximately positions 8–20.
 High-impression / low-CTR pages.
 Declining pages and content decay.
 Query expansion opportunities where a page ranks for relevant topics it barely addresses.
 Cannibalization between multiple URLs.
 Weak internal-link support.
 CTR-only opportunities that may need metadata rather than a rewrite.
 Consolidation opportunities for overlapping or thin articles.
Suggested scoring concept: Opportunity Score = Demand × Ranking Potential × Page Relevance ×
Business Value × Confidence.

8. Article Analysis Pipeline
9. Retrieve the current article, metadata, blocks, headings, links, images, author information and SEO
settings.
10. Determine the article&#39;s primary topic, search intent, audience, conversion goal, angle and existing
strengths.
11. Map Search Console queries to the page and cluster them by topic and intent.
12. Evaluate intent satisfaction, topical coverage, originality, usefulness, evidence, experience, trust,
freshness and organization.
13. Evaluate technical presentation including title, description, H1, headings, canonical, indexability,
schema, links and image attributes.
14. Analyze internal-link opportunities.
15. Optionally compare the page against competing search results for missing useful topics or presentation
patterns.
16. Generate an evidence-based optimization plan before generating replacement text.

9. Content Change Types
 Metadata: SEO titles, meta descriptions and social metadata.
 Structure: introductions, heading order, paragraph organization, tables of contents and conclusions.
 Additions: missing sections, FAQs, comparisons, examples, definitions, steps, troubleshooting and
pros/cons.
 Trust: author information, sources, methodology, update information, firsthand media and disclosures.
 Internal linking: inbound/outbound contextual links and improved anchor text.
 Consolidation: merge overlapping pages and update internal links.
 Reduction: remove filler, repetition, outdated information and irrelevant sections.

10. AI Editing Rules for Content

RankAudit — Master Content &amp; Product Optimization Plan

 Preserve core meaning, author voice, brand terminology, disclosures, shortcodes, forms, embeds and
tracking code.
 Do not invent tests, customer quotes, statistics, credentials, prices, policies, dates or unsupported
factual claims.
 Prefer section-level patches over full-article replacement.
 Use structured AI output containing analysis, recommendations, additions, modifications, removals,
metadata changes, internal-link changes, verification flags and final patches.
 Require human verification when new factual information cannot be established from trusted data.

11. Content Editing Modes
 Recommendations Only — no WordPress changes.
 Draft Assistant — creates a revised WordPress draft.
 Section-Level Approval — approve, edit or reject each change.
 Automatic Low-Risk Improvements — optional automation for narrowly defined safe changes.
 Full Autopilot — later-stage feature with explicit consent, revision creation, quality checks, change limits
and rollback.

12. Content Performance Tracking
 Record baseline queries, positions, impressions, clicks and CTR before publication.
 Measure at approximately 7, 14, 28, 56 and 90 days.
 Statuses: awaiting data, too early to judge, improving, stable, declining, reverted, needs work and
successful.
 Describe outcomes as correlated improvements/declines rather than claiming every ranking movement
was caused by RankAudit.

completeness, trust, conversion usefulness and structured data rather than merely rewriting descriptions.

 Conversion Usefulness — whether important buying questions are answered near the decision point.
 Data Confidence — whether each claim is verified, imported, manufacturer-sourced, document-sourced,
conflicting or awaiting verification.
